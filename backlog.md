# mostik — backlog

## The goal

An HTTP → Kafka bridge whose status code is a true statement about the record:

- `200` means the record is in the topic at the offset the response names;
- `429` and `503` mean it is not there;
- `504` means nobody knows yet, and a retry may write it twice.

It is built from [keel](https://github.com/youndie/keel) and ships twice, as a Kotlin/Native binary
and as a JVM distribution in one image. It publishes through [kafkakn](https://github.com/youndie/kafkakn),
and it is the first kafkakn consumer that bounds `send` with a deadline. Why the answers are shaped this
way is in [research](docs/research/research-architecture.md).

## The reality check

**Nothing of mostik is built.** The code is keel's template at `youndie/keel@6be238d`, unchanged. B-01
turns it into mostik. The feature, API and service documents are drafted on the branch **docs/drafts**
and are merged as the code gives them anchors.

**Part of this backlog waits on another repository.** The deadline answers depend on kafkakn B-73 and
B-74. Both merged on 2026-09-27 (kafkakn `fe4f1c4`): `enqueue` either throws `RecordNotQueuedException`,
which becomes `429`, or returns a `Delivery` whose cut `await()` becomes `504`, on both arms (research
§1.3, settled). kafkakn republished its snapshot on 2026-09-26 at 22:45 UTC, with both in it. So B-04 waits only on B-01:
mostik has to depend on kafkakn before it can compile against the new calls.

## Stages

| Stage | Name | What closes it |
|---|---|---|
| `stage-1-skeleton` | From keel to a bridge that builds | both builds link against the published kafkakn and start; a broker and a reader exist for the scenarios |
| `stage-2-publish` | The happy path | `200` means "in the topic at that offset", on both builds; `404` and `413` never reach the producer |
| `stage-3-bounded-wait` | The deadline, and what it needs from kafkakn | `429` and `504` are both true statements, proven by reading the topic |
| `stage-4-shutdown` | `SIGTERM` under load | every answer a client got agrees with the topic, and the process ends within its grace period, or the overrun is measured and routed |

A stage closes as a whole and gets a line here saying what came out beyond the plan, and which research
hypothesis was confirmed or refuted.

## Decisions worth not re-litigating

- **`504` means "unknown", with `retrySafe: false`**, not `503`, and there is no idempotency key in the
  first version. The owner chose this on 2026-09-26. Research D2.
- **The deadline is enforced by mostik, not by producer keys.** No library bound is portable across the
  two arms. Research §1.4.
- **What kafkakn has to change is changed in kafkakn**, and this repository takes a republished snapshot
  (B-04). Research D7.
- **No authentication in the service**; the proxy does it, and `TOPICS` is an allowlist. The owner chose
  this on 2026-09-26. Research D4.
