# mostik — backlog

## The goal

An HTTP → Kafka bridge whose status code is a true statement about the record:

- `200` means the record is in the topic at the offset the response names;
- `429` and `503` mean it is not there;
- `504` means nobody knows yet, and a retry may write it twice.

It is built from [keel](https://github.com/youndie/keel) and ships twice, as a Kotlin/Native binary
and as a JVM distribution in one image. It publishes through [kafkakn](https://github.com/youndie/kafkakn),
and it is the first kafkakn consumer that bounds `send` with a deadline. Why the answers are shaped this
way is in [research](docs/research/research-architecture.md).

## The reality check

**The route publishes under a deadline.** B-01 made the skeleton from keel's template. B-02 added the broker
and the reader. B-03 added `POST /topics/{topic}/records`: `200` with the offset the broker gave, and `404` and
`413` before the producer. B-05 added the deadline: `429` when the record was provably never queued, and `504`
when the deadline wins after queueing. Every answer was checked by reading the topic, on both builds. B-07 refuses a drain shorter than the deadline. B-08 measured the shutdown with the broker gone: kore cuts `close` at 3 s and the process exits in 8 s. B-09 ran 40 rounds of `SIGTERM` under load: zero disagreements between the clients' ledgers and the topic. B-12 made
the JVM build answer `503` through the announce, as native does (Ktor's JVM hook, youndie/kore#90). B-10 made
a busy port a one-line refusal. B-11 found the reset connections at shutdown to be the listening socket's accept queue closing with it, in a window already
answering `503`. What is open: the question in B-06.

**Part of this backlog waited on another repository, and no longer does.** The deadline's answers rested on
kafkakn B-73, B-74 and B-76. All three are published in kafkakn `0.1.0.11`, which B-04 pins.

hypothesis was confirmed or refuted.

## Decisions worth not re-litigating

- **`504` means "unknown", with `retrySafe: false`**, not `503`, and there is no idempotency key in the
  first version. The owner chose this on 2026-09-26. Research D2.
- **The deadline is enforced by mostik, not by producer keys.** No library bound is portable across the
  two arms. Research §1.4.
- **What kafkakn has to change is changed in kafkakn**, and this repository takes a republished snapshot
  (B-04). Research D7.
- **No authentication in the service**; the proxy does it, and `TOPICS` is an allowlist. The owner chose
  this on 2026-09-26. Research D4.
